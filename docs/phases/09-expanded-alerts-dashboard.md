# Phase 09 — Expanded alerts and local dashboard

Status: IMPLEMENTED AND OFFLINE VERIFIED. No live feed or Telegram delivery was used in validation. No profitability claim.

## Delivery

- The dynamic CEX scan still caps at 150 exact instruments and deep analysis at 30. The scan and deep selection now reserve up to 12 eligible liquid spot instruments; if fewer pass catalog freshness, turnover and spread checks, fewer are analyzed. This makes room for popular spot assets and exchange-listed memecoins without treating tickers as cross-venue identity. Five deep instruments are rotated per 15-second cycle under the existing request budget.
- Beyond legacy SOL/HYPE, a disciplined shadow plan can produce an experimental Telegram spot alert. It requires a fresh observed card, exact venue/quote identity, fresh book and closed 15m/1h/4h evidence, fresh BBO and a valid 60-second plan TTL. The plan already freezes entry, stop, target, fees, slippage and risk. Alerts are capped at four per hour and 20 per day, with a persisted at-most-once attempt before network delivery. They are not calibrated win probabilities, executable orders, or confirmed profitable signals. Perpetual short plans remain excluded.
- High-impact normalized events can produce source-linked Telegram news explanations. They require credibility >=0.7, no authenticity/contradiction risk, and arrival within one hour. Each message names exact impacted canonical assets (or the whole market for macro/regulatory events), states a conditional impact scenario, and links primary evidence. An observed retraction/contradiction of a delivered event triggers one correction. The cap is 12 per day. Event scenarios do not feed model probabilities.
- A separate report worker checks new on-chain candidates by exact `chain_id + contract`. It reports only an `ELIGIBLE` assessment with official identity, verified pool, fresh paper-exit quote, sufficient proven exit capacity and no risk flags, once per identity and at most six per day. The report is a watch item, not a launch-price claim or buy instruction. Solana/Base RPC workers and quote enrichment remain disabled by default; therefore real new-token reports require suitable read-only RPC, exact pool configuration, and sufficient safety evidence. No paid feed was enabled.
- A standalone read-only dashboard starts on `http://127.0.0.1:8081` by default, separate from Telegram Mini App. It displays whitelisted market cards, source-linked events and exact-contract candidates, with responsive layout, fast 20-second polling, search/filter, no-cache headers and restrictive CSP. Binding to a public interface is rejected until authentication is designed. No raw DB, token, credential or execution endpoint is exposed.

## Verification and limits

Synthetic SQLite tests cover the normal BONK spot cycle through the alert callback, exact identity, freshness, caps, deduplication, news retraction, new-token safety gates, public dashboard field whitelist and HTTP responses. The full offline unit suite passed 255 tests and the integration suite passed 6, with dotenv and pytest cache disabled; each command finished within 60 seconds. Tests never send Telegram messages or place trades.

Selection and setup scores remain heuristics. A larger watchlist does not itself improve forecast accuracy. Official news sources currently cover only configured feeds; project/exchange feeds must be added through documented APIs or RSS. On-chain reports cannot run live while RPC providers and verified pools are disabled. The dashboard is local only. Forward out-of-sample paper results with complete fees, spread, slippage, latency, exposure and quote gaps are required before any claim of profitability or model promotion.
