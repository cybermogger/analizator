# Phase 09 — Expanded alerts and local dashboard

Status: IMPLEMENTED AND OFFLINE VERIFIED. No live feed or Telegram delivery was used in validation. No profitability claim.

## Delivery

- The dynamic CEX scan still caps at 150 exact instruments and deep analysis at 30. The scan and deep selection now reserve up to 20 eligible liquid spot instruments; if fewer pass catalog freshness, turnover and spread checks, fewer are analyzed. Within the spot reserve, selection first favors distinct base symbols before extra venue/quote pairs. This prioritizes broader popular and exchange-listed memecoin coverage without using a symbol as cross-venue asset identity. Five deep instruments are rotated per 15-second cycle under the existing request budget.
- Beyond legacy SOL/HYPE, a disciplined shadow plan can produce an experimental Telegram spot alert. It requires a fresh observed card, exact venue/quote identity, fresh book and closed 15m/1h/4h evidence, fresh BBO and a valid 60-second plan TTL. The plan already freezes entry, stop, target, fees, slippage and risk. Alerts are capped at four per hour and 20 per day, with a persisted at-most-once attempt before network delivery. They are not calibrated win probabilities, executable orders, or confirmed profitable signals. Perpetual short plans remain excluded.
- High-impact normalized events can produce source-linked Telegram news explanations. They require credibility >=0.7, no authenticity/contradiction risk, arrival within one hour, and publication within 24 hours. This prevents old feed items from being sent as breaking news on first import. Each message names exact impacted canonical assets (or the whole market for macro/regulatory events), states a conditional impact scenario, and links primary evidence. An observed retraction/contradiction of a delivered event triggers one correction. The cap is 12 per day. Event scenarios do not feed model probabilities.
- The official Ethereum Foundation RSS feed is enabled and tied to the Ethereum project identity. Explicit mainnet upgrade announcements can trigger an ETH news scenario with unknown price direction; testnet-only upgrade articles are not treated as high-impact mainnet news. Feed polling is read-only and failure-isolated. The endpoint was verified as HTTP 200 XML; no live feed was polled by the application during tests.
- A separate report worker checks new on-chain candidates by exact `chain_id + contract`. It reports only an `ELIGIBLE` assessment with official identity, verified pool, fresh paper-exit quote, sufficient proven exit capacity and no risk flags, once per identity and at most six per day. The report is a watch item, not a launch-price claim or buy instruction. Solana/Base RPC workers and quote enrichment remain disabled by default; therefore real new-token reports require suitable read-only RPC, exact pool configuration, and sufficient safety evidence. No paid feed was enabled.
- The official CEX catalog also reports newly observed spot pairs after each provider/scope has a persisted complete baseline. A first run never labels existing pairs as new. A later exact venue pair must have fresh catalog data, at least one million native quote units of 24-hour turnover and a spread no wider than 50 bps; it may become eligible later within 24 hours of discovery. Reports are capped at two per scope refresh and six per day, with at-most-once attempts. The wording explicitly says a pair is new **to this scanner**, not that a token launched or will rise.

## Verification and limits

Synthetic SQLite tests cover the normal BONK spot cycle through the alert callback, exact identity, freshness, caps, deduplication, news retraction, Ethereum mainnet versus testnet classification, diverse spot selection, new-token safety gates, catalog baseline/new-pair/partial-scope routing, public dashboard field whitelist, virtual journal and HTTP responses. The offline unit suite passed 261 tests and the integration suite passed 6, with dotenv and pytest cache disabled; each command finished within 60 seconds. Tests never send Telegram messages or place trades.

Selection and setup scores remain heuristics. A larger watchlist does not itself improve forecast accuracy. Official news sources currently cover only configured feeds, including Ethereum Foundation, not all listed assets; project/exchange feeds must be added through documented APIs or RSS. On-chain reports cannot run live while RPC providers and verified pools are disabled. The dashboard is local only. Forward out-of-sample paper results with complete fees, spread, slippage, latency, exposure and quote gaps are required before any claim of profitability or model promotion.
