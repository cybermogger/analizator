# Phase 7A — Public CEX discovery

> **Current checkpoint:** 7A discovery and Stage 7B descriptive analysis are present in the uncommitted worktree. Stage 7C causal snapshots and observational outcomes are implemented in this worktree and await final verification. Mini App changes remain separate uncommitted paths and must not be included in any later analytics commit.

## Objective and boundary

Add bounded, public catalog discovery for liquid CEX markets to the existing async service. Phase 7A does not alter the SOL/HYPE signal models, add news-driven ranking, connect on-chain candidates to the CEX universe, or enable trading. Hyperliquid remains the derivatives provider and the only input venue for the established SOL/HYPE production analysis. Multi-venue truth and discovered-universe output are diagnostic/shadow data.

## Configured sources

All endpoints are public, official, read-only, and configured under `providers` in `config.yaml`:

| Provider | Catalog scopes | Catalog/quote path |
|---|---|---|
| Binance | Spot and USDⓈ-M perpetuals | `exchangeInfo` + `ticker/24hr`; bulk `ticker/bookTicker` per scope |
| Coinbase Advanced Trade | Spot | Cursor-paged public market products; bounded per-product public book requests |
| Kraken Spot | Spot | `AssetPairs` + `Ticker`; one bulk ticker refresh |
| Hyperliquid | Existing configured perpetual instruments | Existing adapter; dynamic catalog discovery is not used |

The Coinbase calls use its explicitly public routes under `/api/v3/brokerage/market/`; the authenticated `/brokerage/products` and `/brokerage/product_book` routes are not used. Coinbase pagination is read from the documented nested `pagination.has_next` and `pagination.next_cursor` fields, and the catalog remains incomplete unless a terminal page is observed. Kraken's returned `wsname`, `altname`, and result pair key are preserved as exact venue metadata. Its documented `XXBTZUSD`/`XBT/USD` representation maps to the explicitly configured Bitcoin instrument; no generic symbol aliasing is applied. See the official [Coinbase public product list](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/advanced-trade-api/rest-api/public/list-public-products), [Coinbase public product book](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/advanced-trade-api/rest-api/public/get-public-product-book), [Kraken AssetPairs](https://docs.kraken.com/api-reference/market-data/get-tradable-asset-pairs), and [Kraken Ticker](https://docs.kraken.com/api-reference/market-data/get-ticker-information) specifications.

HTTP timeout is at most 8 seconds in the checked-in config and adapters reject values above 10 seconds. Coinbase catalog pages are bounded (250 products/page, at most 20 pages, configured delay); an incomplete/empty page set is degraded and cannot replace the prior catalog. Catalog refresh defaults to 900 seconds with a 1800 second freshness limit. A failed scope is quarantined as stale; it does not mark the scope's instruments delisted. Only a successful complete scope can deactivate instruments that disappeared from that scope.

## Identity and quote safety

Each venue instrument is keyed by provider, venue, market type, base, quote, and venue symbol. Existing canonical identity is retained only on an exact configured venue-identity match. Other CEX instruments receive a deterministic venue-scoped `unresolved:` identity; a ticker match alone never joins two providers or contracts. Configured SOL/BTC venue mappings remain explicit. Exchange-native aliases are not guessed.

No USD/USDT/USDC FX conversion is configured. Turnover and top-of-book depth remain in the instrument's quote units. Market truth groups observations by the exact quote currency, requires distinct-provider quorum, and marks other currency groups uncomparable. If independent quote groups each form quorum, or two sources disagree beyond the safe threshold, canonical price is suppressed/degraded rather than silently combining them.

## Universe selection and budgets

The scan candidate budget is at most 150 instruments and the deep watchlist at most 30. Candidate baskets are `majors_l1`, `derivatives_movers`, `liquid_spot_candidates`, and a reserved `dex_memecoin_candidates` bucket. The DEX bucket has no CEX catalog producer and remains empty until a separately approved chain integration supplies eligible candidates.

Before BBO enrichment, the service excludes inactive products, unsupported quote currencies, configured fiat/stable bases, absent turnover, and turnover below `market_catalog.minimum_quote_turnover_24h` (default `$1,000,000` in the quote currency). Ranking uses turnover, the absolute 24-hour price-change magnitude (a movement proxy, not statistical volatility), freshness, derivatives turnover, and measured spread/depth. Binance `closeTime` is checked for staleness/future skew; Coinbase catalog and Kraken REST ticker do not provide a per-product exchange timestamp and therefore retain receive time only as catalog lineage, not exchange time. Invalid per-market quote metrics exclude that market with a reason while preserving a complete catalog identity; malformed/truncated scope pagination remains incomplete and cannot delist prior instruments. A fresh BBO is required before final membership; spread above `maximum_spread_bps` (default 50) fails closed. BBO request strategy is provider-aware: Binance/Kraken bulk calls are gated as one request; per-product requests are individually gated by provider concurrency and minimum-spacing limits. Coinbase enrichment is capped by its `bbo_enrichment_limit` (default 10). Deep L2 and trade requests are limited to the selected deep watchlist.

## Persistence and health

SQLite schema v13 adds `market_discovery_decisions`, keyed by instrument, with the latest admission/exclusion/delisting/stale decision, reasons, source URL, catalog receive time, evaluation time, and measured quote-turnover/spread/liquidity metrics. It also adds immutable `analysis_observations` and restart-safe `analysis_outcomes`; existing rows are retained. Catalog reconciliation deactivates absent markets without deleting their rows.

## Known limitations / not implemented in this phase

- X Filtered Stream and broader official/news feeds remain separately configured and currently disabled pending account/source setup.

- Newly discovered CEX tickers are not cross-provider merged until a verified canonical mapping is supplied; no ticker-only aliases are inferred.
- CEX REST catalog and BBO discovery is not the same as continuous CEX WebSocket market truth. Existing CEX WebSocket endpoint settings do not imply that their feeds are active in the analysis model.
- Event and on-chain evidence do not contribute to production ranking or the current SOL/HYPE ensemble.
- The Phase 4 model card remains `NOT_EVALUABLE` without a reproducible causal real-market snapshot; no challenger is promoted by this phase.
- Public endpoint availability, rate limits, and regional reachability remain deployment-specific. No paid API or account tier is assumed.

## Review remediation notes

- Binance 24h change is a signed exchange metric; the catalog stores only its explicitly named absolute-move proxy and uses a separate finite signed parser. Positive price/size parsing remains non-negative.
- Coinbase traversal follows the official nested pagination object. Missing pagination metadata, empty pages, repeated cursors, or hitting the configured page cap produce an incomplete scope, which the service must not reconcile as a successful catalog.
- Quote quality is evaluated per market row. Invalid/non-positive/crossed BBO values, when supplied by the catalog endpoint, exclude the affected market with a reason; an omitted BBO remains eligible for the later bounded bulk-BBO enrichment. Invalid turnover excludes that instrument from ranking but does not invalidate an otherwise complete listing catalog. Binance exchange timestamps are checked against configured age and future-skew limits.

## Phase 7B — Descriptive analysis of deep CEX instruments

### Implemented scope

- `MultiAssetAnalysisService` rotates over at most 30 existing deep-watchlist venue instruments; each cycle takes a bounded slice and caps candle history/cache, request count, per-request timeouts and provider concurrency through the Phase 7A request gate.
- Candle history is kept in a dedicated in-memory cache keyed exactly by `(instrument_id, interval, open_time_ms)`; it does not pass through `MarketTruthEngine`'s latest-event filter. Only closed, non-future, interval-aligned bars are admitted. Duplicate keys preserve the later local receive snapshot, while out-of-order bars are ordered by open time for computation. The analysis uses the trailing contiguous closed-bar run and reports stale, gap, open/future exclusion and insufficient-history state without filling missing values.
- For 5m/15m/1h/4h, sufficient history yields trend, four-bar momentum, per-bar realized log-return volatility and relative volume. A multi-timeframe agreement fraction is emitted only when at least two interval trends are usable. These values and the coverage/agreement score are descriptive, not calibrated forecasts or win probabilities.
- The card includes exact provider/venue/market type/base/quote/venue symbol, source receive age, fresh BBO and L2 spread/top-five native-quote displayed depth/imbalance. Displayed depth is explicitly not executable capacity. Funding and OI are queried only for perpetual instruments whose adapter implements `DerivativesDataProvider`; unsupported combinations stay unavailable.
- Provider and instrument failures are isolated; one call has the configured provider HTTP timeout clamped to 10 seconds and the analysis worker rotates instead of requesting all candle histories every 15 seconds. Health exposes analyzed/deep sizes, bounded-cache/request settings, cycle/failure counts and last receive age.
- The normal daemon starts/stops the independent worker. Russian `/markets` and `/analyze <exact-instrument-id>` read its cached cards; ticker-only input is rejected as ambiguous. Legacy `/sol`, `/hype`, existing ensemble probabilities, paper decisions and Mini App are unchanged.

## Phase 7C — Causal observation snapshots and bounded outcomes

- Normal bounded analysis cycles save at most one immutable snapshot per exact venue instrument, feature version/configuration and cadence bucket (default 300 seconds). Snapshots preserve contract/canonical identity, base/quote/symbol, observation and record times, exact BBO-mid reference, spread, exchange/receive/availability times, point-in-time features/quality/lineage, frozen trend-only baseline and the deep-universe membership/reasons. Future or non-causal source timestamps are excluded from feature/context payloads.
- The snapshot captures exact configured costs (per-side fees/slippage and one explicitly counted quoted-spread round-trip term); it does not invent funding. Outcome rows for 15m/1h/4h are created idempotently and persist across restart and later universe removal. Only the exact same venue instrument and quote can resolve an outcome. A BBO whose exchange event and local receive are outside the target-to-deadline window is rejected; after the configured max latency it becomes terminal `UNAVAILABLE`.
- Gross reference return, trend-baseline costs-adjusted observational return and actual trading PnL are separate concepts; the latter is never produced. Missing references, pending outcomes and unavailable/delisted-source outcomes remain visible as cohort statuses. `/analysis_status` and `/analysis_performance` report coverage and outcomes. There is no training or promotion, and real predictive quality remains `NOT_EVALUABLE` until enough causal real observations accrue.
- No event/on-chain joins are fabricated: snapshots say `NO_VERIFIED_CONTEXT` unless a future exact canonical and causal join is implemented. No X, RPC, paid provider, exchange execution, daemon start, or Telegram delivery was added.

### Phase 7C review remediation

- Source clocks now distinguish `EXCHANGE_EVENT`, `RECEIVE_TIME_FALLBACK`, and `DERIVED_INTERVAL_CLOSE`. A receive-only endpoint is never relabeled as an exchange event merely because its adapter had to populate a timestamp field. Receive-only BBOs may supply a fresh local reference, but the stored exchange timestamp is `NULL` and provenance is explicit. Known event timestamps must be positive, not future, and within the applicable freshness window; book/funding/OI feature groups with a future event or receipt timestamp are excluded.
- Target BBO validation samples local time after the provider await. The observation's immutable target and deadline, the BBO exchange-event time when known, local receive time, and post-await current time are checked independently. An outcome can only be `OBSERVED` inside the frozen target window; a missed/expired outcome is terminalized from SQLite without consuming a network request. When the in-memory registry is empty after restart, only the exact stored provider/venue/type/base/quote/symbol/contract identity is reconstructed. One unavailable market does not block sibling outcomes.
- Maximum live work defaults to 8 exact `(instrument, quote)` groups per 15-second poll, with a hard service cap of 20. One current BBO can be evaluated against multiple due horizons for that same exact venue instrument/quote; each row independently enforces its immutable target/deadline and may remain pending if the quote falls outside its window. Due outcomes are processed at the start of a cycle before candle/derivative work, and the total cycle budget remains 30; funding/OI only use the budget left after due-outcome requests and selected candle requests. At the configured 30-market, three-horizon, 300-second cadence, conservative unsquashed demand is estimated at 18 quote groups/minute and default capacity at 32/minute (1.78x nominal margin). Expired work is batched separately from live requests. Capacity status and live/expired queue sizes are visible in health and `/analysis_status`; the 60-second target deadline remains strict.
- Every outcome freezes its cost policy at snapshot time (fee/slippage per side, quoted-spread accounting method, cadence, target deadline and policy version). Runtime setting changes do not rewrite existing cohorts. Reports group by feature version/config hash and frozen cost policy; gross reference return, long-reference net return and trend-baseline gross/net remain distinct. Trend baseline ties are explicit no-position rows (no fabricated zero-cost trade); directional zero-gross observations still pay round-trip costs. Counts show each metric denominator, zero-return rows, no-position rows, unavailable/missing-reference and delisted coverage. These are observational references, never execution or profitability claims.
- Universe snapshots persist the complete bounded candidate membership and selection reasons available to the service, plus catalog-scope and provider-error lineage; each observation points to that snapshot. If provider discovery has not supplied a full candidate snapshot, the exact available membership is recorded as such rather than inventing excluded candidates.
- Regression coverage includes a real short async delay with a post-await clock advance for 15m/1h/4h, receive-only provenance, stale/future-source exclusion, frozen fee policy (4 bps remains 4 after runtime changes to 40), zero-gross cost accounting, no-position denominators, exact-identity restart reconstruction, failed/healthy neighbor isolation, and a synthetic 30-market four-hour catch-up: 90 same-target horizon rows collapse to 30 instrument requests, resolved in four bounded cycles at 8 requests/cycle.
